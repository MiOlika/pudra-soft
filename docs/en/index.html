Clip Ink
Description

Clip Ink is a lightweight screen recorder for Windows. Move and resize the frame over the area you need, click "Start recording", and get a regular MP4 that opens in any player and uploads to any platform. The frame itself stays out of the video: only what is inside it gets recorded.

Record a custom area, the whole screen, or an app window, with sound from the microphone, the system, or both. You can draw and add captions while recording.

  • Regular MP4 (H.264 + AAC) for any player or platform
  • Your recording is kept if the app closes unexpectedly
  • Free disk space monitoring
  • Interface in Russian and English
  • Runs on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit)

Frequently asked questions

What is the maximum recording length?

One video can be up to 20 minutes long. When the limit is reached, the recording is saved automatically and you can start the next one right away.

Can I turn sound on and off while recording?

Yes, you can mute and unmute the microphone and system audio during recording without stopping it.

Why does part of the video record as black?

Hardware-accelerated content (some games, DRM-protected video) may be recorded as black.

Is there echo cancellation?

No. If you record both the microphone and system audio, use headphones.

Version 1.0.0.1 for Windows

Film Ink
Description

Film Ink is a video editor for Windows that makes it easy to view, edit, and join videos. Drag files in from Explorer, mark the parts you need in the source monitor, and assemble your video on the timeline: video and images on track V1, music on A1. The whole edit plays back right away in the preview, no rendering needed.

For each clip you can set speed, volume, fade in and fade out, brightness, contrast, and saturation, and rotate or flip the frame. Save the finished video as MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM, or GIF, or export just the audio as MP3 or WAV. FFmpeg is built in, so there is nothing else to install.

  • Preview the whole edit without rendering
  • Up to 100 undo and redo steps, projects saved as .fink files
  • Export from SD to 4K, plus vertical 9:16 and square 1:1
  • Built-in FFmpeg and automatic updates from GitHub
  • Interface in Russian and English, light and dark themes

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install FFmpeg or codecs separately?

No. FFmpeg and the player are built into the app, so everything works right after installation.

Which formats can I export to?

MP4, MKV, and MOV (H.264), WebM (VP9), and GIF, plus audio only as MP3 or WAV. Resolution can match the source or range from SD to 4K, including 9:16 and 1:1 for social media. You can also save the current frame as a PNG.

Where are projects and exported videos stored?

In "Documents\Pudra Soft\Film Ink": projects, exports, frame snapshots, and settings are all kept there.

Why does Windows show a warning during installation?

The installer is not digitally signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn about it. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

What an MVP is and why you need one

An MVP is a minimum viable product. Its goal is to test the key hypothesis with the smallest possible set of features. Start with the main user task, release a first version, and gather feedback before adding functionality.

How to manage your working time

Identify priority tasks, record the time you actually spend, and regularly compare the plan with the result. Time tracking is not about total control; it helps you understand your workload and find ways to improve.

How to organize team work

Define the project goal, break the work into clear tasks, and assign owners. Update statuses regularly and discuss obstacles. A shared board helps the team see progress and keep track of agreements.

Why analyze the time you spend

Match time against task types and project stages. Look at trends over weeks, not just individual days. This helps you estimate deadlines more accurately, distribute the workload, and improve processes.
